Gobal average anemia prevalence

-The global average anemia prevalence decreased from about 31% in 2000 to nearly 28% in 2023, indicating an improvement of women’s nutritional and health status in many countries.

Distribution of Anemia prevalence in 2000 vs 2023

  • The median anemia prevalence has decreased, and the interquartile range has widened, meaning inequality among countries might have increased.

Average Maternal Mortality ratio from 2000 to 2023

  • Maternal mortality has declined globally, from about 156 per 100,000 in 2000 to roughly 96 in 2023, a substantial improvement.

mmr by continent

Relationship between Anemia and MMR

  • There is a weak positive correlation. Countries that reduced MMR also tended to reduce anemia prevalence, but many outliers exist. For example, some countries saw anemia rise despite stable MMR.

Comparison Table between Anemia and MMR

  • This interactive table displays a side‑by‑side comparison of anemia prevalence and maternal mortality ratio (MMR) for each country, covering the years 2000 and 2023. Additional columns include absolute changes for both indicators, allowing users to sort, search, and identify countries that have improved or worsened. The colour‑coded bars visually highlight the magnitude of change.

Key takeaways

  • Maternal mortality has fallen globally, however anemia has slightly worsened, and regional gaps remain wide.
  • Anemia and MMR are weakly correlated – Countries that improve one often improve the other, but exceptions highlight the need for targeted nutrition and health‑system interventions.
  • Data empowers action – Transparent, interactive dashboards help policymakers identify high‑burden countries and allocate resources effectively.
